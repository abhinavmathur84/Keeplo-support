Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 (Keeplo 2.0)

Keeplo – Photo Agent ("the App", "we", "us"), formerly Declutter – Photo Cleaner, is developed by Abhinav Mathur. This Privacy Policy explains what the App accesses on your iPhone, how it is used, and your choices. In short: your photos stay on your iPhone, the App has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.

1. Photos and videos

The App asks for access to your Photo Library (through Apple's Photos framework) to find duplicates and similar shots, rank files by size, search your photos, build collages, cards and reels, and delete, shrink or archive items at your direction. All analysis happens on your iPhone. We never upload your photos, videos, thumbnails or their metadata to us. Nothing is deleted without your approval, and deletions can be undone for a short time.

2. On-device AI

Search, the Cleanup Assistant and Studio use Apple's Vision framework and, on iPhones that support it, Apple Intelligence on-device models. These run on your iPhone. On other iPhones the App uses built-in rules instead. Your search text and Studio prompts are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

3. Place names (Apple geocoding)

Holiday Memories can show a place name for a trip. A built-in list of popular destinations is checked first. If needed, the App asks Apple's geocoding service (part of iOS) to convert a place name you typed, or the centre coordinate of a group of photos, into a place label. This request goes to Apple and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. It is not sent to us. The App never asks for your device's live location.

4. Sharing and printing, only when you choose

If you share a creation or choose a print service, the App hands your file to the iOS share sheet or opens the print service's website or app, only after you tap and confirm. The App saves print-ready images to an album in your Photo Library for that purpose. What happens next is governed by the service you choose. The App keeps a local log on your device of what you shared, so you can review it.

5. Data stored on your device

The App stores settings and small amounts of data on your device only, for example: cumulative space saved and items deleted, items you marked "Looks Fine", a library index used to speed up search, your preferences, the local "shared" log, and local usage counters used to tune the App's features. This data is never uploaded and is not shared with anyone. Deleting the App removes it.

6. What we do not do

  • No account or login is required.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party SDKs.
  • The App works offline.

7. Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. It has no social features and no user accounts.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If a future version ever sends data off your device, this policy will say so before that version is released.
