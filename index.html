Common questions

Does Keeplo upload my photos? No. Cleanup, search and the Studio run on your iPhone. Photos leave the phone only when you choose to print or share, and you approve exactly which ones.

Can I undo a deletion? Yes — every delete has an Undo, and iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Why does it say "Smart presets"? On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, Keeplo uses fast built-in rules instead of the on-device AI model.

When does it analyse my library? Mostly while your iPhone is charging, so it doesn't drain your battery. You can turn this off in Settings.
